Biological Sourcing Assessment: In Review

Form-1 SBB Clearance Required

Pursuant to Sections 3 and 4 of the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) Act 2002, commercial utilization of biological resources harvested from Indian natural habitats mandates prior intimation to the concerned State Biodiversity Board (SBB). While cultivated Ashwagandha qualifies under domestic standard commercial channels, wild-sourced Tinospora cordifolia (Guduchi) triggers mandatory Form-1 clearance before patent prosecution.
